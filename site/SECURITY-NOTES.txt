Lavender Arcade HTTP/HTTPS compatibility notes

- The site does not require service workers, WebAuthn, geolocation, clipboard,
  camera, microphone, WebUSB, WebBluetooth, or other secure-context-only APIs.
- Static browsing and game catalog filtering work on HTTP.
- Security headers that remain meaningful on HTTP are enabled in .htaccess.
- HSTS is emitted only when HTTPS is active.
- No third-party scripts, analytics, fonts, or CDNs are required.
- Do not put login credentials, payment data, age-verification secrets, or
  creator-upload processing on plain HTTP.
- Accounts, payments, authenticated creator uploads, and sensitive APIs should
  require HTTPS even though the public static catalog can degrade to HTTP.
