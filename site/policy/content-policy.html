Policy

Content & Age Policy

Lavender Arcade separates age eligibility from regional availability. Age categories control which games a user may browse. Regional policy rules control which content categories the platform makes available in a given jurisdiction.

Age bands

  • Under 13: family-safe catalog; no mature violence, sexual content, real-money gambling, illegal-drug themes, or unrestricted social features.
  • 13–16: teen catalog with stronger fantasy/action themes and supervised communication features only.
  • 17+: expanded action, survival, and mature themes where permitted by platform policy.
  • 18+: mature catalog only after age-gating. Adult status does not override intellectual-property, malware, exploitation, gambling, controlled-substance, or other legal/platform restrictions.

Regional rules

The file data/jurisdiction-rules.json is a configuration layer. It should be reviewed by qualified counsel before being used as a statement of actual law. The site should not infer age from location.

Emulator/homebrew policy

Lavender Arcade may support original homebrew software, authorized ports, and public-domain games. It should not distribute unauthorized copyrighted ROMs or keys.
