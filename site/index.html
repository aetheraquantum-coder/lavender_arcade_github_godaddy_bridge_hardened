Creator program

Publish your own game

Creators can submit original games, authorized ports, public-domain titles, and lawful homebrew packages. The live production version should quarantine and scan every upload before publication.

Allowed

  • Original games and artwork
  • Lawful homebrew builds
  • Authorized ports
  • Public-domain content
  • Age-rated mature content that passes platform policy

Not accepted

  • Stolen or unauthorized commercial ROMs
  • Games whose distribution creates clear legal liability
  • Content blocked by the active platform policy or jurisdiction rule set
  • Uploads containing malware or hidden trackers
